Pioneer Car Service Center · External retail customer journey

One journey, three service tiers, two payer paths, three exception routes.

A single consolidated map of the retail customer journey. It reads in three registers: which stages apply to each service tier, the core process spine with the self-pay and insurance routes shown as separate lanes, and the exception journeys that branch off the spine and rejoin it on resolution.

Register 1 — Service tier

The band above the lanes shows which of the eleven stages each tier touches. A routine service skips diagnosis, quotation and approval entirely; a major repair uses every stage.

Register 2 — The spine

Eight lanes across eleven stages. The payer route forks at triage in stage 02 and rejoins at settlement in stage 10. Insurance steps are shown in blue.

Register 3 — Exceptions

Three off-ramps below the spine, each labelled with the stage it branches from: quote revision, rework and comeback, and refund or dispute.
